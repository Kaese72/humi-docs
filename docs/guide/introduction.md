# Introduction

Humi is a home automation system. It has two parts:

- **The Appliance** runs in your home. It talks to your devices (Philips Hue,
  IKEA Dirigera, …), keeps track of their state and runs your automation
  rules. Everything works without the Cloud.
- **Humi Cloud** is optional. It lets you reach your Appliance from outside
  your home and share it with the people in your Group.
