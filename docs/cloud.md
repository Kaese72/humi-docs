# Cloud

Humi Cloud is optional. Your Appliance works without it; the Cloud adds a
way to reach your Appliance from outside your home and to share it with
other people.

## Groups

Everything in the Cloud belongs to a **Group**. When you create an account
you get a Group of your own, and you can invite other people into it.
Everyone in a Group can reach the Appliances that belong to it.

## Appliances

An Appliance is connected to the Cloud by enrolling it into one of your
Groups. Once enrolled, you can open it through the Cloud from anywhere,
and log in to it with your Humi Cloud account.
